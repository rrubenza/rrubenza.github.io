blurb:    "The Sun is our best hope to understand how stellar variability impacts our observed spectra. It is the only star for which we can truly isolate *only* the (stochastic) stellar and instrument signals from the (known, deterministic) planet signals.\n\nMy current work at the CCA combines a year of solar spectra from KPF, NEID, HARPS-N, and EXPRES to trace solar variabiltiy and instrumental drift *per pixel.* For the Sun, that can be mapped to formation layers in the atmosphere. For drift, to location on the detector. Stay tuned for the paper coming very soon!"
